Printing tips
  1. In the print window, choose the ZD411 printer.
  2. Paper size should read 1 x 0.5 in, margins None, scale 100%.
  3. Turn off "Headers and footers" if it is shown.
  4. Click Print. Wrong output? Tell your supervisor, do not hand-correct the label.
